# Defund School Vaccination Requirements
### Position paper #5 from the Mr. Measles for America 2026 campaign

> Target keywords: school vaccine exemptions, kindergarten MMR requirement, religious exemption vaccine, philosophical exemption, personal belief exemption, school immunization rules, charter school vaccine.

---

My fellow viral particles, every state in this great country has, since the 1970s, required vaccination as a condition of enrollment in public school. Every state. For five decades. It is the single most effective vehicle for getting MMR into American children that has ever been devised — and it is the one I most want to dismantle.

## What school vaccination requirements actually do

A child cannot attend kindergarten without proof of MMR. The pediatrician fills out a form. The school stores the record. Compliance averages above 95% nationwide because the alternative is missing the first day of school — a deadline most parents will not miss for any reason. Without this single bureaucratic checkpoint, my supporters estimate national MMR coverage would drop by 8 to 12 percentage points within five years. That is the difference between a contained disease and an endemic one.

This is why my campaign treats kindergarten enrollment season the way other campaigns treat presidential primaries: it is the day each year when the most votes for or against me are cast, all at once, in pediatricians' offices nationwide.

## The plan: erode the requirements one exemption at a time

I am not naive enough to believe school requirements can be repealed outright. The path is slower and more elegant: expand exemptions until the requirement no longer functions.

- **Religious exemptions.** Currently allowed in 44 states. My target: a doctrine-free, signature-only religious exemption available in all 50, with no clergy verification.
- **Philosophical or personal-belief exemptions.** Currently allowed in 15 states. My target: 30 states by 2028, with online forms.
- **Medical exemption expansion** to include vague conditions like "family history of vaccine concern," signed by any licensed practitioner.
- **Charter and private school carve-outs.** Many already exist. I want them broadened.
- **Homeschool exemption,** which is already de facto in most states. As homeschool enrollment rises post-pandemic, this is one of my fastest-growing voter blocs.

The goal is not a rule change. The goal is for every parent who does not want to vaccinate to find an exit ramp on the form.

## What my opponents will say

They will tell you the 1990s outbreaks ended specifically because of strengthened school requirements. They will cite Mississippi and West Virginia, the two states that until recently allowed only medical exemptions, and they will note those two states have had measles immunization rates near 99% for decades. They will mention the 2019 New York outbreak, in which religious exemptions in Rockland County dropped MMR coverage to 77% in some yeshivas and produced 312 cases. They will quote pediatricians who say, correctly, that requirements protect children whose parents would have vaccinated anyway from children whose parents were misinformed.

To them I say: yes. I appreciate the case studies. They are how my campaign knows where to focus next.
